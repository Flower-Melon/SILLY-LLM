# TASK ALLOCATION SOLUTION

## Step 1: Analyze Subtasks, Skills, and Mass Requirements

**SubTask 1: Place an Egg on the Plate**
- Skills Required: `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`
- Objects involved: Egg (mass 0.055), Plate (mass 0.62)
- Max mass handled: 0.62 (Plate is picked up? No — only Egg is picked up; Plate is a receptacle). Actually only Egg is picked up → mass = 0.055

**SubTask 2: Place a Tomato on the Plate**
- Skills Required: `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`
- Objects involved: Tomato (mass 0.12), Plate (receptacle)
- Mass handled: 0.12

**SubTask 3: Microwave the Plate**
- Skills Required: `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`, `OpenObject`, `CloseObject`, `SwitchOn`, `SwitchOff`
- Objects involved: Plate (mass 0.62), Microwave (mass 7.0, not picked up)
- Mass handled: 0.62 (Plate is picked up)

## Step 2: Analyze Robot Skills and Mass Capacity

| Robot | Skills | Mass Capacity |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| robot1 | GoToObject, OpenObject, CloseObject | 100 |
| robot2 | GoToObject, SwitchOn, SwitchOff | 100 |
| robot3 | GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject | 100 |
| robot4 | GoToObject, SliceObject, PickupObject | 100 |

All robots have mass_capacity = 100, which far exceeds any object mass (max 7.0). **Mass is not a constraint** — focus on skills alone.

## Step 3: Skill Matching

**SubTask 1 (Egg on Plate)** — needs `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`
- robot3 has all three ✓
- robot4 has GoToObject, PickupObject but lacks PutObject ✗
- **Assign to robot3**

**SubTask 2 (Tomato on Plate)** — needs `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`
- robot3 has all three ✓
- **Assign to robot3**

**SubTask 3 (Microwave Plate)** — needs `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`, `OpenObject`, `CloseObject`, `SwitchOn`, `SwitchOff`
- No single robot has all 7 skills:
  - robot1: GoToObject, OpenObject, CloseObject (3/7)
  - robot2: GoToObject, SwitchOn, SwitchOff (3/7)
  - robot3: GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject (3/7)
  - robot4: GoToObject, PickupObject, SliceObject (2/7)
- **Skill gap → form a team**
- Team {robot1, robot2, robot3} covers: GoToObject, OpenObject, CloseObject, SwitchOn, SwitchOff, PickupObject, PutObject = all 7 ✓
- Team {robot1, robot2, robot4} lacks PutObject ✗
- **Assign SubTask 3 to team {robot1, robot2, robot3}**

## Step 4: Dependency & Execution Order

- SubTask 1 and SubTask 2 are **independent** → can run in **parallel**
- SubTask 3 **depends on** SubTask 1 and SubTask 2 (plate must contain egg and tomato first) → must run **sequentially after** both

## Step 5: Minimum Robots & Scheduling

- SubTask 1 → robot3
- SubTask 2 → robot3
- SubTask 3 → team {robot1, robot2, robot3}

Since robot3 is needed for SubTask 1, SubTask 2, and SubTask 3, and SubTask 1/2 both require robot3, they **cannot truly run in parallel** (shared robot). Serialize them on robot3.

**Minimum robots needed: 3** (robot1, robot2, robot3). robot4 is not needed.

## Final Allocation

```
SubTask 1 (Egg on Plate)      → robot3          [sequential]
SubTask 2 (Tomato on Plate)   → robot3          [sequential, after ST1]
SubTask 3 (Microwave Plate)   → {robot1, robot2, robot3}  [after ST1 & ST2]
```

**Execution plan:**
1. robot3: place egg on plate
2. robot3: place tomato on plate
3. Team {robot1, robot2, robot3}: microwave the plate
   - robot3: GoToObject(Plate), PickupObject(Plate), GoToObject(Microwave), PutObject(Plate, Microwave), PickupObject(Plate), GoToObject(CounterTop), PutObject(Plate, CounterTop)
   - robot1: OpenObject(Microwave), CloseObject(Microwave) (open/close around insertion and removal)
   - robot2: SwitchOn(Microwave), SwitchOff(Microwave)

**Robots used: 3 (robot1, robot2, robot3)** — the minimum required, since no single robot possesses all skills for SubTask 3, and robot3 is the only robot capable of the pickup/put subtasks.
