# Task Allocation Solution

## Task Decomposition

**SubTask 1: Turn on the Sink faucet**
- Skills Required: `GoToObject`, `SwitchOn`
- Objects involved: Sink (mass 0.0), Faucet (mass 0.0)

**SubTask 2: Put toilet paper in the trash**
- Skills Required: `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`
- Objects involved: ToiletPaper (mass 0.2), GarbageCan (mass 0.7)

**Dependency Analysis:** SubTask 1 and SubTask 2 are independent (no shared objects, no ordering constraint), so they can be executed **in parallel**.

---

## Robot Skill & Mass Analysis

| Robot | Skills | Mass Capacity |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| robot1 | GoToObject, OpenObject, CloseObject | 100 |
| robot2 | GoToObject, SwitchOn, SwitchOff | 100 |
| robot3 | GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject | 100 |
| robot4 | GoToObject, SliceObject, PickupObject | 100 |

All robots have a mass capacity of 100, which far exceeds the mass of any object involved (max 0.7). **Mass is not a constraint here** — allocation is driven purely by skills.

---

## Subtask-to-Robot Matching

**SubTask 1 (Turn on Sink faucet)** requires `GoToObject` + `SwitchOn`:
- robot1: ❌ (no SwitchOn)
- robot2: ✅ (GoToObject, SwitchOn)
- robot3: ❌ (no SwitchOn)
- robot4: ❌ (no SwitchOn)
- **→ Assigned to robot2**

**SubTask 2 (Put toilet paper in trash)** requires `GoToObject` + `PickupObject` + `PutObject`:
- robot1: ❌ (no PickupObject/PutObject)
- robot2: ❌ (no PickupObject/PutObject)
- robot3: ✅ (GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject)
- robot4: ❌ (no PutObject)
- **→ Assigned to robot3**

No team formation is needed — each subtask is fully satisfied by a single robot's skill set, and mass is well within capacity.

---

## Execution Plan

Since the two subtasks are independent and assigned to **different robots**, they can run **in parallel** with no resource contention.

```python
import threading

def turn_on_sink_faucet():
    # SubTask 1 — robot2
    GoToObject('Sink')
    SwitchOn('Faucet')

def put_toilet_paper_in_trash():
    # SubTask 2 — robot3
    GoToObject('ToiletPaper')
    PickupObject('ToiletPaper')
    GoToObject('GarbageCan')
    PutObject('ToiletPaper', 'GarbageCan')

task1_thread = threading.Thread(target=turn_on_sink_faucet)   # robot2
task2_thread = threading.Thread(target=put_toilet_paper_in_trash)  # robot3

task1_thread.start()
task2_thread.start()
task1_thread.join()
task2_thread.join()
```
