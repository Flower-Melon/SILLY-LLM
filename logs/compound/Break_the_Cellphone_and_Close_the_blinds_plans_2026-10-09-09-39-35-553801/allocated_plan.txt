# Task Allocation Solution

## Task Analysis

**Task Description:** Break the Cellphone and Close the blinds

### Subtask Decomposition

| Subtask | Skills Required | Object | Object Mass |
|---------|----------------|--------|-------------|
| SubTask 1: Break the Cellphone | GoToObject, BreakObject | CellPhone | 0.16 |
| SubTask 2: Close the Blinds | GoToObject, CloseObject | Blinds | 0.0 |

**Dependency Analysis:** These two subtasks are **independent** — breaking the cellphone does not affect closing the blinds. They can be executed **in parallel**.

## Robot Capability Analysis

| Robot | Skills | Mass Capacity |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| robot1 | GoToObject, OpenObject, CloseObject | 100 |
| robot2 | GoToObject, BreakObject, SliceObject, SwitchOn, SwitchOff, PickupObject, PutObject, DropHandObject, ThrowObject, PushObject, PullObject | 100 |
| robot3 | GoToObject, SliceObject, PickupObject | 100 |

---

## Skill Matching

**SubTask 1 — Break the Cellphone** (needs `GoToObject`, `BreakObject`):
- robot1: ❌ missing `BreakObject`
- robot2: ✅ has both `GoToObject` and `BreakObject`
- robot3: ❌ missing `BreakObject`
- **→ Assigned to robot2**

**SubTask 2 — Close the Blinds** (needs `GoToObject`, `CloseObject`):
- robot1: ✅ has both `GoToObject` and `CloseObject`
- robot2: ❌ missing `CloseObject`
- robot3: ❌ missing `CloseObject`
- **→ Assigned to robot1**

---

## Mass Capacity Check

- CellPhone mass = 0.16 ≤ robot2 capacity (100) ✅
- Blinds mass = 0.0 ≤ robot1 capacity (100) ✅

No teams are required — each subtask is fully satisfiable by a single robot.

---

## Execution Plan

Since the subtasks are independent and each is assigned to a **different** robot, they can run **in parallel** with no resource contention.

```python
import threading

def break_cellphone():
    # SubTask 1 — assigned to robot2
    GoToObject('CellPhone')
    BreakObject('CellPhone')

def close_blinds():
    # SubTask 2 — assigned to robot1
    GoToObject('Blinds')
    CloseObject('Blinds')

task1_thread = threading.Thread(target=break_cellphone)   # robot2
task2_thread = threading.Thread(target=close_blinds)      # robot1

task1_thread.start()
task2_thread.start()
task1_thread.join()
task2_thread.join()
```

**Minimum robots used: 2** (robot1 and robot2). robot3 is not needed because it lacks both `BreakObject` and `CloseObject` skills, and no team formation is necessary since each subtask is fully covered by a single robot. The two subtasks are independent, so they execute in parallel on separate robots for maximum efficiency.
