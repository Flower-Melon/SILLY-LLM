# Task Allocation: Slice the Tomato

## 1. Task Decomposition

**Main Task:** Slice the tomato

**SubTask 1:** Slice the Tomato
- **Skills Required:** `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `SliceObject`, `PutObject`
- **Objects involved & masses:**
  - Knife: 0.18
  - Tomato: 0.12
  - CounterTop: 0.0
- **Max object mass handled:** 0.18 (Knife)

There is only one subtask, so no parallelization is possible — it must be executed as a single sequential unit.

## 2. Robot Capability Analysis

| Robot | Skills | Mass Capacity |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| robot1 | GoToObject, OpenObject, CloseObject, BreakObject, SliceObject, SwitchOn, SwitchOff, PickupObject, PutObject, DropHandObject, ThrowObject, PushObject, PullObject | 100 |
| robot2 | (same 13 skills) | 100 |
| robot3 | (same 13 skills) | 100 |

**Skill check:** All three robots possess every required skill (`GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `SliceObject`, `PutObject`).

**Mass check:** The heaviest object the robot must physically handle is the Knife (0.18). All robots have mass_capacity = 100 ≫ 0.18. No mass gap exists.

## 3. Allocation Decision

- All robots are **skill-equivalent** and **mass-equivalent** for this task.
- Since the subtask is a single indivisible sequence (pick up knife → slice tomato → return knife), it cannot be split across robots without breaking the physical continuity of holding the knife.
- **Minimum number of robots needed = 1.**

**Assign SubTask 1 → robot1** (any of the three would work; robot1 chosen as the first available).

## 4. Execution Plan

```
Sequential execution (single robot, single subtask):

robot1:
  GoToObject('Knife')
  PickupObject('Knife')
  GoToObject('Tomato')
  SliceObject('Tomato')
  GoToObject('CounterTop')
  PutObject('Knife', 'CounterTop')
```

No teams are required, no parallelization is possible, and no dependencies exist beyond the internal ordering of the subtask itself.

| SubTask | Skills Required | Mass Requirement | Assigned To | Mode |
|---------|-----------------|------------------|-------------|------|
| Slice the Tomato | GoToObject, PickupObject, SliceObject, PutObject | ≤ 0.18 | robot1 | Sequential (single robot) |
