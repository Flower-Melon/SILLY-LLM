# TASK ALLOCATION SOLUTION

## Task Analysis

**Task Description:** Place the Bar of soap in the sink first and then place dishsponge in the sink

### Subtask Decomposition

**SubTask 1:** Place the Bar of soap in the sink
- **Skills Required:** GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject
- **Objects involved:** SoapBar (mass = 0.11), Sink (mass = 0.0)
- **Max mass to handle:** 0.11

**SubTask 2:** Place the dishsponge in the sink
- **Skills Required:** GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject
- **Objects involved:** DishSponge (mass = 0.03), Sink (mass = 0.0)
- **Max mass to handle:** 0.03

### Dependency Analysis

The task explicitly states "**first** and **then**", meaning SubTask 2 depends on the completion of SubTask 1. Therefore, the subtasks **must be performed sequentially**, not in parallel.

---

## Robot Capability Analysis

All three robots have **identical skill sets** (13 skills each) and **identical mass capacity** (100 each):

| Robot | Required Skills Present? | Mass Capacity | Handles SoapBar (0.11)? | Handles DishSponge (0.03)? |
|-------|--------------------------|---------------|--------------------------|-----------------------------|
| robot1 | ✅ GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject | 100 | ✅ | ✅ |
| robot2 | ✅ GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject | 100 | ✅ | ✅ |
| robot3 | ✅ GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject | 100 | ✅ | ✅ |

**Key observations:**
- All robots share the same skills and mass capacity → allocation is driven by **minimizing the number of robots used**.
- No skill gap exists → no team formation needed.
- No mass gap exists (0.11 and 0.03 ≪ 100) → no team formation needed.

---

## Allocation Decision

Since the subtasks are **sequential** (SubTask 2 must wait for SubTask 1), and any single robot can perform both subtasks, the **minimum number of robots required is 1**.

**Assign both subtasks to `robot1`**, executed sequentially:

1. `robot1` performs SubTask 1 (place SoapBar in Sink).
2. After SubTask 1 completes, `robot1` performs SubTask 2 (place DishSponge in Sink).

Using a single robot satisfies the minimum-robot requirement and respects the sequential dependency. Robots 2 and 3 remain idle.

## Execution Code

```python
import threading

def place_soap_in_sink():
    # SubTask 1: Place the Bar of soap in the sink
    GoToObject('SoapBar')
    PickupObject('SoapBar')
    GoToObject('Sink')
    PutObject('SoapBar', 'Sink')

def place_dishsponge_in_sink():
    # SubTask 2: Place the dishsponge in the sink
    GoToObject('DishSponge')
    PickupObject('DishSponge')
    GoToObject('Sink')
    PutObject('DishSponge', 'Sink')

# Sequential execution on robot1 (minimum robots = 1)
# SubTask 1 must complete before SubTask 2 begins
place_soap_in_sink()          # robot1
place_dishsponge_in_sink()    # robot1 (after SubTask 1)
```
