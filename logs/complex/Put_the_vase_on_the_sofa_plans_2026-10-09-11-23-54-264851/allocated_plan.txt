# Task Allocation Analysis: Put the Vase on the Sofa

## 1. Task Decomposition

**Main Task:** Put the vase on the sofa.

**SubTask 1:** Put the vase on the sofa.
- **Skills Required:** `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`
- **Objects involved:**
  - Vase (mass = 1.0) — must be picked up
  - Sofa (mass = 103.99) — receptacle, not picked up
- **Mass requirement:** The robot must be able to lift the **Vase (mass = 1.0)**. The Sofa is only a placement target, so its mass is irrelevant to the robot's carrying capacity.

## 2. Robot Capability Analysis

| Robot | Skills | Mass Capacity | Has Required Skills? | Can Lift Vase (1.0)? |
|-------|--------|---------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| robot1 | GoToObject, OpenObject, CloseObject, BreakObject, SliceObject, SwitchOn, SwitchOff, **PickupObject**, **PutObject**, DropHandObject, ThrowObject, PushObject, PullObject | 0.4 | ✅ Yes | ❌ No (0.4 < 1.0) |
| robot2 | GoToObject, OpenObject, CloseObject, BreakObject, SliceObject, SwitchOn, SwitchOff, **PickupObject**, **PutObject**, DropHandObject, ThrowObject, PushObject, PullObject | 0.9 | ✅ Yes | ❌ No (0.9 < 1.0) |

## 3. Skill & Mass Gap Analysis

- **Skill check:** Both robots possess all three required skills (`GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject`). ✅
- **Mass check:** The Vase has mass **1.0**. Neither robot individually has sufficient mass capacity (robot1 = 0.4, robot2 = 0.9). ❌

This is a **mass gap** — no single robot can perform the subtask alone.

## 4. Team Formation

Per the allocation rules, when a subtask cannot be performed by a single robot due to mass capacity, form a team whose **combined mass capacity** meets the requirement.

- **Team:** {robot1, robot2}
- **Combined skills:** Union of both skill sets → includes `GoToObject`, `PickupObject`, `PutObject` ✅
- **Combined mass capacity:** 0.4 + 0.9 = **1.3 ≥ 1.0** ✅

The team of robot1 and robot2 collectively satisfies both the skill and mass requirements.

## 5. Execution Order

There is only **one subtask**, so no parallelization is possible. The team must execute the subtask **sequentially/cooperatively**:

1. `GoToObject('Vase')` — navigate to the vase
2. `PickupObject('Vase')` — jointly lift the vase (combined capacity 1.3 ≥ 1.0)
3. `GoToObject('Sofa')` — navigate to the sofa
4. `PutObject('Vase', 'Sofa')` — place the vase on the sofa

## 6. Final Allocation

| SubTask | Skills Required | Mass Required | Assigned To | Reason |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|-------------|--------|
| Put the vase on the sofa | GoToObject, PickupObject, PutObject | 1.0 (Vase) | **Team: robot1 + robot2** | Neither robot alone has mass capacity ≥ 1.0; combined capacity 1.3 ≥ 1.0 |

**Minimum number of robots used: 2** (both available robots are required — this is the minimum feasible team).

**Execution mode:** Sequential cooperative execution by the team (single subtask, no parallelism possible).
